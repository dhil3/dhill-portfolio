Spark Advertising

Digital presence development & marketing management

Starting point
Spark needed consistent digital activity to communicate its printing and advertising services and make them easier to discover online.
Objective
Establish consistent platform activity, communicate printing services, develop promotional creatives, maintain Google Business Profile, and review early performance insights.

Agreed scope completed

  • Social media management on Facebook and Instagram (~5–10 posts, ~1–3 reels published)
  • Creative content production: flex and banner printing creatives, service carousels and festival visuals
  • Google Business Profile management: profile information maintenance, photos and review monitoring
  • Performance monitoring: review of Instagram and Google Business Profile analytics

How I Work

A Practical Six-Step Workflow

Six repeatable stages. Open any stage to see what happens and what I need from you.

01UnderstandLearn about the business, services, audience and objectives.

What happens

Review current Google listings, website, social channels and competitor communication.

Deliverables

Presence assessment, visibility gaps and priority action list.

What I need from you

Existing account access, core services list, service areas and target customer profile.

02PlanIdentify content themes, platform priorities and publishing needs.

What happens

Define content pillars, publishing cadences and promotional campaign themes.

Deliverables

Monthly content calendar, visual brand approach and caption framework.

What I need from you

Upcoming promotions, seasonal offers and common customer questions.

03CreateDevelop graphics, videos, captions and promotional material.

What happens

Design posts, carousels, short-form reels, promotional headlines and clear calls to action.

Deliverables

Social media creative batch, captions and platform-ready export files.

What I need from you

Timely review and approval of the creative batch.

04ManagePublish content and maintain the relevant digital platforms.

What happens

Schedule approved posts, update Google Business Profile and coordinate brand presence.

Deliverables

Consistent platform publishing and accurate, up-to-date business information.

What I need from you

Notice of any temporary changes to hours, services or pricing.

05MonitorReview available insights, audience activity and content performance.

What happens

Track post views, interactions, review feedback and incoming message activity.

Deliverables

Drafted review responses and prompt notification of qualified enquiries.

What I need from you

Guidance on technical questions or sensitive customer queries.

06ImproveUse evidence and business feedback to refine future activity.

What happens

Evaluate content-type results, review platform metrics and identify content opportunities.

Deliverables

Monthly performance review and prioritized adjustments for the next cycle.

What I need from you

Real-world feedback on walk-ins, phone inquiries and closed orders.

Reporting & Evidence

Clear Reporting, Without Inflated Claims

Results are assessed against the business’s starting point, agreed objectives, available data and the period of activity.

  • Reach and impressions are reported separately from clicks and enquiries.
  • Content format comparisons (reels vs carousels vs static) guide ongoing publishing decisions.
  • Revenue is only discussed when reliable tracking supports it.
  • Missing or unavailable data is explained, not estimated.

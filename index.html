Process

How a Project Runs

Six repeatable stages. Open any stage to see what happens and what I need from you.

01Audit & DiscoverReview business presence, audience and competitors.

What happens

Current Google listings, website, social channels and local competitors.

Deliverables

Presence audit, visibility gaps and a priority action list.

What I need from you

Account access where it exists, core services, service areas and target customer.

02Establish FoundationImprove profiles, information and contact pathways.

What happens

Google Business Profile, categories and hours, branded social profiles and contact channels.

Deliverables

Optimised profiles and consistent name, address and phone details.

What I need from you

Verification support, logo files, official contact details and business photos.

03Plan the StrategyDefine content pillars, priorities and calendar.

What happens

Content themes, audience needs and a monthly publishing rhythm.

Deliverables

Content calendar, caption approach and social brand guidance.

What I need from you

Upcoming promotions, seasonal priorities and common customer questions.

04Create & PublishDevelop creatives and publish planned content.

What happens

Posts, carousels, short-form video, headlines and calls to action.

Deliverables

Scheduled social posts, Business Profile updates and captions.

What I need from you

Timely review and approval of each content batch.

05Engage & MonitorReview audience activity and customer feedback.

What happens

Reviews, comments, messages and enquiries, within the agreed scope.

Deliverables

Drafted review replies, audience responses and passing leads to you promptly.

What I need from you

Guidance on technical questions and sensitive topics such as pricing.

06Analyse & ImproveEvaluate available data and refine the plan.

What happens

Search and Maps discovery, reach, link clicks, call clicks and engagement.

Deliverables

Monthly report, content review and priorities for the next cycle.

What I need from you

Real-world feedback such as walk-ins and enquiries received.

Reporting

Clear Reporting, Without Inflated Claims

Results are assessed against the business’s starting point, agreed objectives, available data and the period of activity.

  • Reach and impressions are reported separately from clicks and enquiries.
  • Revenue is only discussed when reliable tracking supports it.
  • Missing or unavailable data is explained, not estimated.
